### 🔬 About

I'm a researcher and ML engineer with a dual degree (M.Sc. Biological Sciences + B.E. Electrical & Electronics) from **BITS Pilani**. I currently work as an **AI Engineer at TELUS Digital** in Bengaluru, and I'm looking to take my research further in a PhD.

The problems I care about share one theme: **how processes at one level of a biological system give rise to behaviour at another.** Amino-acid sequences shape molecular recognition; networks of neurons produce rhythm and computation. I want to use data to uncover the rules connecting the two levels, with models grounded in the physics and biology of the systems themselves.

### 🧭 Research Interests

- 🧬 **Protein language models & molecular recognition:** predicting protein–protein interaction sites, transfer across proteomes
- 🧠 **Neural circuit dynamics:** rhythm generation, synchronization, and computation in neuronal networks
- 🔍 **Interpretable, mechanistic modelling:** models that explain, not just predict

### 🧪 Research Highlights

**Protein–protein interaction prediction (H1N1)** · *Computational Structural Biology Group, BITS Pilani*
Co-led the first published method for predicting PPI sites on the influenza A proteome, benchmarking classical ML against a Prot-BERT–ANN model. On held-out influenza A proteins the best model reached a mean recall of **0.53 vs 0.18** for the D-SCRIPT baseline, and it transferred to the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein at **55% accuracy** with no coronavirus data in training. Tackled severe class imbalance with SMOTE, positive-unlabeled learning, class weighting, and threshold tuning.

**Respiratory rhythm generation** · *Breathing Neuroscience Lab, NCBS-TIFR*
Analyzed in vivo mouse electrophysiology (LFP power spectra, multi-unit activity) from the preBötzinger Complex, contributing to the finding that the preBötC oscillates faster than breathing and that network synchronization gates the transition from burstlets to propagating inspiratory bursts.

**Multi-epitope vaccine design (*P. aeruginosa*)** · *Genetics & Molecular Microbiology Lab, BITS Pilani*
Built the reverse-vaccinology pipeline (NetCTL, IEDB, VaxiJen, AllerTOP, ToxinPred) that shortlisted 8 CTL, 8 HTL, and 4 B-cell epitopes from four outer membrane proteins, then modelled and docked the construct to TLR4 (Robetta, GalaxyRefine, ClusPro). Worked with the wet-lab team on expression, purification, and biophysical validation.
